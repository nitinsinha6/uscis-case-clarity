How it works

Three steps from sign-in to a clearer update.

  1. 1

    Sign in to myUSCIS

    Open your official myUSCIS account and navigate to the page showing your cases.

  2. 2

    Open the extension

    The extension detects supported receipt numbers or lets you add one manually.

  3. 3

    Run a manual check

    Review a readable summary and verify important details in the official USCIS notice.

FAQ

Important questions

Is this an official USCIS product?

No. Case Clarity is an independent, unofficial developer tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by USCIS or DHS.

Does it submit anything to USCIS?

No. The extension is designed only to read case-response information after the user manually starts a check.

Can I rely on it for appointment details?

No. Always use the official USCIS notice for the appointment date, local time, location, identification requirements, and legal effect.

Does the website process payments?

No. This GitHub Pages website links to external hosted payment or support pages configured by the site owner.
